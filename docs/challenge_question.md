Identify data-driven energy business use cases — measured in €/ton.

A use case can be anything energy related: from better forecasting over trading all the way to investment decisions.
Should be as grounded in their current situation as possible.
Decide what you'd ask Henkel for in the first on-site visit.

The single most load-bearing data request.
The single most load-bearing stakeholder you need 30 minutes with.
The thing we actually evaluate
Method overweighs outcomes.

We do not care if your numbers are right. We care about how you got there. Why this assumption and not the other one. Why this use case in the shortlist and the other one cut. Why you'd run with €X/ton even though the source says €Y. Why you used the tool you used.

A great submission could be six paragraphs and a single back-of-envelope spreadsheet — if the reasoning is clean. A bad submission could be 30 pages of tables — if we can't tell what you actually thought.

What is allowed
Everything. Any tools. Any LLMs. Any code. Any libraries. Any data you can find. Use RIZM's public material (rizm.de). Use whatever lets you think faster.

One condition: be explicit about what you used and where. We want to see your toolchain, not hide it.

How we evaluate
The scorecard expectations for this role are public. Read them before you start — that's how you find out what we look for and what stops us.

Submit single files or a public GitHub/Notion/PDF link.

Attach up to 10 files (≤ 2 MB each) via this page.
