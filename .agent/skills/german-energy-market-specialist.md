# German Energy Market Specialist Skill

## Market Rules & Tariff Structure
1. **Wholesale Power Markets (SMARD / ENTSO-E):**
   - Day-Ahead Spot Auction (hourly / 15-min price signals).
   - Intraday Continuous Market (15-min resolution, key for short-term renewable balancing).
2. **Natural Gas & Carbon Taxes:**
   - Trading Hub Europe (THE) gas benchmark (~€35–45/MWh).
   - EU ETS Carbon Price (€80–120/ton CO2) added to natural gas combustion emission factors (0.201 tCO2/MWh_gas).
3. **Grid Fee Regulations (§ 19 Abs. 2 StromNEV):**
   - **Intensive Netznutzung:** >7,000 full-load operating hours/year AND >10 GWh annual consumption unlocks an 80–90% discount on grid usage fees (*Nutzungsentgelte*).
   - **Atypische Netznutzung:** Load shifting away from distribution system operator (DSO) peak load windows.
   - **Critical Constraint:** Arbitrage / load-shifting dispatch MUST NOT break the flat-load band threshold or lower utilization hours below 7,000h, risking €2M+ in lost discounts.
